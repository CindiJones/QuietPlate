SUPPORT & RESOURCES

You do not have to sort this out alone.

Self-reflection can help, and sometimes human support is the most useful next step.

◉

Registered dietitian

Can help with satisfying meal patterns, restriction, cravings, and individual nutrition needs. Look for someone with experience in eating behaviour or eating disorders.

＋

Physician

Can explore health factors that may affect appetite or food thoughts and connect you with appropriate care.

♡

Therapist or counsellor

Can support you with stress, emotions, compulsive patterns, body image, or the role food plays in coping.

∞

Eating disorder support

If food thoughts feel distressing or connected to disordered eating, a local eating disorder organization can help you find specialized, compassionate support.

i

A note about this app

This app provides educational and self-reflection tools only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If your thoughts about food feel distressing, compulsive, or connected to disordered eating, please speak with a qualified health professional.
